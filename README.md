# Privacy Policy for GPS Savage

**Last updated:** January 2026

GPS Savage respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the GPS Savage mobile application (“the App”).

---

## Information We Do Not Collect

GPS Savage does **not** collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

Specifically, the App does **not** collect:
- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account information
- User identifiers or profiles
- Contacts, photos, or media
- Payment or financial information
- Advertising identifiers
- Analytics or tracking data
- Crash reports or diagnostic data

---

## Location Information

GPS Savage uses **real-time location data** solely to provide navigation functionality.

- Location data is processed **locally on your device**
- Location data is **not stored**
- Location data is **not transmitted off the device**
- Location data is **not shared with any third parties**
- Location data is **not used for tracking or advertising**

You may enable or disable location access at any time through your device settings.

---

## Voice and Audio

GPS Savage uses on-device speech synthesis to provide spoken navigation guidance.

- All spoken commentary is generated locally on the device
- No voice recordings are collected or stored
- No audio data is transmitted off the device

---

## Third-Party Services

GPS Savage uses Apple-provided system frameworks, including:
- Apple MapKit
- Apple Core Location
- Apple AVFoundation

These services operate under Apple’s privacy policies. GPS Savage does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or external AI services.

---

## Data Storage

- Saved locations (if used) are stored **locally on your device**
- No user data is backed up to external servers
- No cloud synchronization is performed

---

## Children’s Privacy

GPS Savage does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the App. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

---

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the App Store listing.

---

**GPS Savage is designed to be privacy-first and fully functional without collecting personal data.**
